Who am I?

I'm Martina, a Multimedia Design student passionate about UX/UI and visual design. I've always known that I wanted my work to have a purpose: creating something meaningful and genuinely useful. Design allows me to do that while giving me the freedom to express my creativity.

I think that the recipe for great design is more than just aesthetic, it's about creating solutions that are visually engaging while solving real problems in the easiest and most intuitive way possible. I love taking on new challenges, and finding the balance between beauty and functionality is what makes me the most excited in the design process.
